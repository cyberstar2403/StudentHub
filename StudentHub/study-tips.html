STUDY WITH PURPOSE

Better habits, built one session at a time.

You do not need a perfect routine. You need a few reliable techniques you can return to when the semester gets busy.

A SIMPLE PRINCIPLE

Make the work visible.

“Study more” is hard to act on. “Solve five practice questions” gives you a clear beginning and end.

Build a weekly plan →
01

FOCUS & TIME

Work in focused blocks

Choose one task, remove obvious distractions, and work for a realistic interval — perhaps 25 or 40 minutes. Take a short break, then decide whether to continue. Use the first two minutes to write down exactly what “done” means for that block.

  • Keep your phone out of reach if it interrupts you.
  • Group small admin tasks instead of switching constantly.
  • Stop to stretch and rest your eyes between blocks.
02

NOTE-TAKING

Capture ideas, not every sentence

Listen for the main claim, supporting explanation, worked examples, and questions. After class, rewrite the key points in your own words. Add a small diagram or example when it makes a relationship clearer.

  • Use headings to show the structure of a topic.
  • Mark confusing areas with a question to revisit.
  • Summarise each section in one or two sentences.
03

REVISION

Use spaced practice and active recall

Return to material over several days rather than leaving all revision until the night before. Begin each review by trying to recall the ideas without looking, then check your answer and correct mistakes.

  • Turn headings into questions.
  • Mix recall with practice problems.
  • Schedule a short review after one day, then a few days later.
04

EXAM PREPARATION

Practise under realistic conditions

Use past questions or create your own prompts. Set a time limit occasionally, show your working, and review errors by type: misunderstanding, forgotten fact, careless step, or time pressure.

  • Start preparation early enough to ask for help.
  • Prioritise weak topics, not only comfortable ones.
  • Protect sleep in the days before an exam.
05

SUSTAINABLE PRODUCTIVITY

Plan around your actual energy

Place demanding work in the parts of the day when you tend to focus best. Keep a short “next actions” list so it is easier to restart after classes or other commitments.

  • Leave buffer time for tasks that take longer than expected.
  • Break large assignments into visible milestones.
  • Review your plan weekly and adjust without guilt.
